Why I Build in the Open

When I started learning Java, I thought the goal was to know everything before showing anything. Open source taught me the opposite. The first time I opened a pull request, I was terrified someone would see how much I didn't know. Instead, a maintainer left a kind comment, fixed my formatting, and merged it.

That moment changed how I learn: I stopped waiting to be ready. Now I build side projects in public, write notes when I figure something out, and treat every bug as a lesson worth sharing. Teaching and open source aren't separate from being a developer for me — they're how I get better at it.

If you're starting out too: ship the imperfect thing. Someone out there needs to see that it's possible.
